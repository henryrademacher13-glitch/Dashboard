05 Questions

Straight answers

How much do I need to spend?

$100 a day, about $3,000 a month. Below that Meta never gets enough data to optimise.

Are the leads exclusive?

Yes. They come from your own ad account, go straight to your CRM, and are never resold.

How fast will I see estimates?

First leads within days, first booked estimates inside two weeks. Month three usually pays best.

Who makes the ads?

Our in-house editor. You send phone footage against a shot list we give you.

Am I locked in?

90 days to start, because ads need that long to prove out. After that, month to month.

Do I need a website?

No. We build the landing page the ads point to.
