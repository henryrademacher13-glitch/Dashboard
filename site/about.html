About

A small agency in Philadelphia, running ads for contractors everywhere.

We build and run Facebook and Instagram ad systems for contractors — roofers, HVAC companies, remodelers and the trades around them. We take on a short list of clients at a time, and we run one channel properly instead of six badly.

SEC. 01 Who we are

Small on purpose

Contractor.Ads is a small agency. That is not an apology — it is the whole operating model. A media buyer who is responsible for forty accounts is checking yours on a Tuesday if something breaks. We keep the roster short enough that every account gets looked at daily, by the same people who built it.

Strategy, copy, media buying and creative all sit under one roof, including our own in-house editor. Nothing gets handed to a freelancer who has never sold a roof, and nothing gets left on your desk to finish at nine at night.

SEC. 02 Why contractors

Contracting is close to the ideal case for paid social

One job usually pays for a month of advertising several times over, so the math has room to work. The buyer is a homeowner making the decision at their kitchen table, not a procurement committee. The work is visual — a finished roof or a gutted-to-tiled bathroom sells itself in six seconds of phone video. And the need is often urgent, which means the job goes to whoever gets to the phone first rather than whoever ranked first.

Most agencies treat contractors as one vertical on a list of twenty, and it shows in the ads. We only do this, so we already know what a service radius is, why your average ticket changes the whole plan, and why a lead at 6pm on a Friday is worth less than the same lead at 9am.

SEC. 03 Philadelphia

Based in Philadelphia. Working across the country.

Philadelphia is a contractor's city. Rows of flat roofs that fail on schedule, heating systems older than the people who own them, and block after block of houses that need something done to them. It is a good place to learn this trade, and it is where we are.

But a Meta campaign does not care where the person running it sits. It cares about the radius around your yard, the zip codes with the housing stock that matches your ticket, and the homeowners inside them. We run campaigns for contractors well outside our own market, and the only geography that matters on the account is yours.

SEC. 04 Why one channel

Meta only, and that is the point

Google captures demand that already exists — someone typing "roof leak repair near me" at eleven at night. That demand is real, and it is also finite and expensive. Meta does something different: it puts your work in front of the homeowner who has been putting the roof off for two years and has not typed anything into anything.

We could sell you a bundle with SEO and Google and a blog nobody reads. Six services getting ten per cent of the attention each is how contractors end up paying a retainer for a monthly report. One channel, run daily, is a better trade.

SEC. 05 How we work

The way the work actually runs

  • A short client roster, so every account is managed daily rather than reviewed monthly.
  • One contractor per trade, per market — we will turn down your competitor.
  • Creative produced in house, refreshed every month before fatigue kills the account.
  • Reporting on cost per booked estimate and jobs closed. Impressions are a diagnostic, not a result.
  • A 90-day initial term, then month to month with 30 days' notice.
  • Everything built in your accounts. If we part ways, the ad account, page, pixel and creative stay with you.
SEC. 06 What we won't do

The short list of things we say no to

  • Sell you shared leads, or resell yours to anyone else.
  • Boost a post and describe it as an advertising campaign.
  • Put reach and impressions at the top of a report to pad it out.
  • Promise a number we cannot back. We would rather have no guarantee on this site than one we would have to argue about later.
  • Run two competing contractors in the same trade and market.
