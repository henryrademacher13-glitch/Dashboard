Privacy policy

This policy explains what Contractor.Ads collects when you use this site or contact us, what we do with it, who else sees it, and how to get it removed. Plain language, no small print at the bottom.

Last updated: September 2, 2026

POLICY 15 clauses

01Who this covers

Contractor.Ads ("we", "us") is a Philadelphia-based advertising agency serving contractors. This policy covers this website and the enquiries that come through it — the booking form, the phone number and the email address published on these pages. It does not cover the separate privacy practices of any client whose own campaigns we run.

02Information you give us

When you book a call, email us or pick up the phone, you give us information yourself. Typically that is your name, business name, phone number, email address, trade, service area, and whatever you tell us about your budget, revenue or current marketing. We only ask for what we need to tell you whether we can help.

03Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this one records basic technical information when you visit: IP address, browser and device type, referring page, the pages you looked at and how long you stayed. This is collected by our hosting provider and any analytics or advertising tools described below, and we use it to understand how the site is performing.

04Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies and pixels are small files placed on your device. We use them to keep the site working, to measure traffic, and to measure the performance of our own advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.

05Advertising and the Meta Pixel

We advertise our own services on Facebook and Instagram, and this site may carry the Meta Pixel and Meta's Conversions API so we can measure which ads lead to enquiries. That means Meta Platforms, Inc. receives information about your visit and may match it to a Meta account. You can control how Meta uses this in your Meta ad preferences and in your device's tracking settings.

Contractor.Ads is an independent business. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an agent of Meta Platforms, Inc.

06How we use your information

  • To answer your enquiry and schedule a call.
  • To prepare a proposal and, if you become a client, to deliver the work.
  • To send you information you asked for, and occasional follow-up about your enquiry.
  • To measure and improve this site and our own advertising.
  • To keep business records and meet legal and tax obligations.

07When we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with other contractors. We do share it with the service providers we use to run the business — scheduling, email, CRM, hosting, analytics and advertising platforms — and only so they can perform that service for us. We will also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect our legal rights.

08How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for as long as we might reasonably do business together, and client records for as long as we are required to keep business and tax records after the engagement ends. You can ask us to delete yours sooner — see clause 09.

09Your choices and your rights

Email Contractor.adsagency@gmail.com and we will action any of the following: send you a copy of what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. You can also reply STOP to any text message or use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. We will not treat you differently for asking.

10California residents

If you live in California, the CCPA as amended by the CPRA gives you the right to know what personal information we collect and why, to request a copy, to request deletion or correction, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise any of these rights, use the contact details in clause 15.

11Visitors outside the United States

We operate in the United States and information you send us is processed here. If you contact us from outside the US, you are consenting to that transfer. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, our lawful basis is your consent for marketing, and legitimate interest or the performance of a contract for everything else.

12Security

We use reputable providers and standard safeguards to protect the information we hold, including encrypted connections to this site. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, and we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your information.

13Children

This site is for business owners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

14Other sites we link to

Our booking link, and any other outbound link on this site, takes you to a service we do not control. Their privacy practices are their own, and we would encourage you to read them.
